📉 Startup budgeting framework for 2026

82% of failed startups cite cash flow problems (JPMorgan Chase Institute). To avoid that:

  • Zero‑based budgeting – justify every operational expense monthly, no automatic rollovers.
  • Fixed vs variable split – keep fixed overhead below 40% of monthly burn.
  • Runway buffer – always maintain 6+ months of cash reserves, especially in volatile markets.
  • 3‑scenario planning – optimistic, base, worst‑case. Build all three in the financial section of your plan.

1. Building in secret – launch a landing page before coding.

3. Hiring too fast – each hire should solve a proven bottleneck.

4. No co-founder agreement – 48% of startup failures involve founder conflict.

5. Raising too early – over‑funding kills discipline.

6. Not knowing unit economics – know LTV:CAC cold.

7. Copying competitors – their strategy fits their stage, not yours.
