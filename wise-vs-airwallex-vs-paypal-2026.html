🏢 What is Airwallex?

Airwallex is a Melbourne‑born fintech (2015) offering a global financial infrastructure platform. It provides multi‑currency business accounts, payment gateways, corporate cards, and treasury management. Airwallex is built for growing businesses that want to eliminate costly currency conversion.[reference:21]

Key strengths: local bank details in 20+ currencies, free local transfers to 120+ countries, and an embedded finance API. It’s a top choice for e‑commerce, SaaS, and platform businesses scaling internationally.[reference:22]

💸 What is Wise?

Formerly TransferWise, Wise is a London‑based company (2011) famous for its transparent, low‑cost international transfers. Wise uses the real mid‑market exchange rate with a small, upfront fee. The Wise Business account lets you hold 40+ currencies, receive local account details (USD, EUR, GBP, etc.), and issue multi‑currency debit cards.[reference:23]

Wise has no monthly subscription for Essential plan; the Advanced plan has a one‑time fee. It’s ideal for freelancers and SMEs that regularly receive and send cross‑border payments.[reference:24]

🅿️ What is PayPal?

PayPal is the veteran (1998) with massive brand recognition. It offers business accounts that accept payments in 25 currencies across 200+ markets. PayPal provides seller protection, invoicing, and mass payout tools. However, its fee structure is complex and expensive for international transactions: base rate 2.9% + fixed fee, plus 1%‑1.5% cross‑border surcharge, and a 3‑4% hidden FX markup.[reference:25][reference:26]

💱 Currency Exchange Rates: Who Gives the Best Deal?

Airwallex offers a markup of 0.5% for major currencies (USD, EUR, GBP) and 1% for others above the interbank rate.[reference:35] Wise uses the true mid‑market rate with a service fee between 0.33% and 0.60%.[reference:36] PayPal adds a 3‑4% markup on top of the mid‑market rate, making it the most expensive for currency conversion.[reference:37]

Verdict: Wise wins for pure currency conversion (true mid‑market rate). Airwallex is a close second with its low 0.5% markup. PayPal is the costliest.

🌍 International Transfers: Speed & Cost

Airwallex provides free local transfers to 120+ countries; SWIFT transfers cost $10‑30 and take 1‑3 days.[reference:38] Wise claims 96% of transfers complete within 24 hours, with fees as low as 0.43%.[reference:39] PayPal international transfers can take 1‑2 days but come with high fees (2.9% + fixed + 1‑1.5% cross‑border).[reference:40]
