🔎 What is Airwallex?

Founded in Melbourne in 2015, Airwallex is a global fintech platform designed to help businesses manage international payments, multi-currency finances, and spend management without traditional banking friction. As of December 2025, Airwallex raised $330 million in Series G funding at an $8 billion valuation, backed by T. Rowe Price and Robinhood Ventures[reference:0]. The platform processes over $235 billion in annualized transaction volume and holds 60+ financial licenses globally, including PCI DSS Level 1 certification.

💳 Airwallex Cards & Spend Management

Airwallex’s Visa corporate cards support 40+ markets and enable spending directly from multi-currency wallets, avoiding foreign exchange fees. Virtual cards can be issued instantly for employees, and physical cards are available in most regions. Expense management includes real-time receipt capture, approval workflows, and integration with Xero/QuickBooks. This is a major boost for finance teams tired of manual reconciliation.

🔗 Integrations (Xero, Shopify, NetSuite + 40+ apps)

Airwallex integrates natively with Xero, QuickBooks, NetSuite, Shopify, Amazon, WooCommerce, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Zapier, and BambooHR. Automatic bank feeds, hourly reconciliation, and embedded payment links turn accounting into a frictionless process.

💬 What Real Customers Say About Airwallex

✅ Positive trends: On Trustpilot (average rating 3.3/5), many users praise the platform’s user-friendliness, fast transaction speeds, and intuitive app. Startups and mid-market firms highlight low FX rates and seamless card issuance. Account managers like "Robin Su" are frequently commended for proactive support.


⚠️ Common complaints: A recurring criticism involves delayed fund transfers (some users report pending payments for 8+ days), unexpected account freezes without clear communication, and hard-to-reach customer service on weekends. Several reviews mention that while the platform is otherwise solid, support response inconsistency remains a weak spot.

📊 Overall sentiment: Balanced. G2 average score is 4.3/5, while Trustpilot is around 3.2–3.5/5 depending on region. Most complaints relate to support rather than core features.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Airwallex cheaper than Wise for business?

It depends. Airwallex charges 0.3%–0.6% FX + SWIFT fees; Wise charges a 0.4%–1% conversion fee. For high-volume flows, Airwallex may win due to negotiation options and bulk pricing.

Does Airwallex have a physical business debit card?

Yes, physical Visa cards are available in most major markets (US, UK, EU, AU, SG). Virtual cards can be issued instantly for employees.

What countries does Airwallex support?

Business registration in more than 19 countries, plus global accounts for receiving payments in 50+ currencies and sending to 207 countries.

Is Airwallex FDIC insured?

Funds are held in partner banks, and the platform complies with local safeguarding rules (e.g., FCA, MAS, AUSTRAC), but FDIC insurance may not apply to all balances.

🏁 Final Verdict: Should You Choose Airwallex?

Airwallex is an excellent choice for digital-first businesses operating globally. It reduces foreign exchange friction, provides enterprise-grade card infrastructure, and offers a free plan that already beats most banks. The main drawbacks revolve around variable customer support and occasional account holds. Nevertheless, Airwallex remains a top-tier neobank for scaling cross-border operations. If you need a robust all-in-one global payment ecosystem, Airwallex is worth it.
