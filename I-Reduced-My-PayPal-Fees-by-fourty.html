I Reduced My PayPal Fees by Switching Payment Platforms

A freelancer's journey from paying 4-6% in hidden fees to saving over 40% with Airwallex.

✨ Real Founder Story

In early 2025, I was running a small digital agency with contractors in Europe, Asia, and the US. Every month, I’d send roughly $15,000 in cross-border payments and receive payments in USD, EUR, and GBP. My go-to platform was PayPal — until I saw my effective fee rate hit nearly 8% on a single international transfer. That’s when I started researching alternatives.

In this article, I’ll break down exactly what I discovered: the real cost of PayPal’s hidden fees, how platforms like Airwallex compares, and how you can reduce your own international payment costs by switching providers.

📉 The Problem

PayPal’s standard domestic rate is 2.9% + $0.30, but international payments add cross-border fees (1.5%+), currency conversion markups (3-4%), and fixed transaction fees. These compound, often reaching 5-8% effective cost on international sales.

✨ The Solution

By switching to a multi-currency account provider like Airwallex, I was able to hold funds in local currencies, avoid automatic conversion fees, and pay only transparent FX markups (0.5-1% for Airwallex).

Why I Started Looking for Alternatives

It started with a $3,200 invoice from a client in Germany. The client paid via PayPal in EUR, and I received only $2,944 in my US bank account — a loss of $256 (8%). PayPal’s fee breakdown showed a cross-border fee, a fixed fee, and a currency conversion markup of 4% above the mid-market rate. That’s when I realized PayPal’s “convenience” came at a steep price for anyone operating globally.
